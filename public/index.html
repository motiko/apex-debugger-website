Debug Salesforce Apex with ease

Apex Debugger is a browser extension that makes Apex debug logs readable. Search across your latest logs, read them in a fast, color-coded viewer, click any Id to open the record, and jump around Setup with keyboard shortcuts.

Features

Everything you need to read Apex logs

Apex Debugger works with your org's debug logs, on its own viewer and on Salesforce's log page. Missing something? Open an issue on GitHub; feedback is welcome.

  • Search in logs

    Search for a string across your latest logs, even while new ones keep arriving, and jump straight to the entry you need.

  • Fast viewer for huge logs

    The log viewer only renders the lines on screen, so logs with 100,000+ lines open and scroll smoothly.

  • Themes

    Light, Dark, Monokai and High contrast, plus a custom color editor. Follows your system's light or dark mode, and applies on Salesforce's own log page too.

  • Clickable Ids

    Every Id in a log becomes a link. Click it to open the record, Apex Class, Trigger, Validation Rule and more.

  • Indent USER_DEBUG

    Pretty-prints debug output that holds an sObject, List, XML or JSON, so nested data is easy to scan.

  • Start, extend or stop logging

    Turn on a 24-hour trace flag for the current user in one click, extend it, or switch it off when you're done.

  • Delete logs safely

    Clear only your logs or all users' logs. Apex Debugger always asks first and tells you how many logs will be deleted.

Keyboard shortcuts

Get around Salesforce without the mouse
